The GM monster registry contains four complete data definitions. The player Journal Bestiary exposes only discovered knowledge. Existing Guard and Mage profiles remain supported.

## 2024 Level One Budgets
Per character: Low 50 XP, Moderate 75 XP, High 100 XP. No 2014 multi-enemy multiplier; the encounter director separately warns when a solo player faces multiple opponents.

## Initial reusable scenarios
- Warehouse vermin: two Giant Rats, 50 XP. Investigate, withdraw, fight.
- Roadside robber: one Bandit and one Goblin Warrior, 75 XP. Negotiate, investigate, withdraw, fight.
- Ruins scouts: two Goblin Warriors, 100 XP. Retrieve the objective, negotiate, withdraw, fight; higher practical solo risk.

The DM may initiate encounters naturally without forced quests or timed automatic spawns. Encounter fixtures validate through the normal battlefield validator. The DM remains free to create fresh story situations.

## Engine
- GM profiles derive the first four verified monster AC, HP, speed, initiative and attacks from the SRD compendium.
- Giant Rat Pack Tactics checks for an active adjacent ally. Goblin Minion Nimble Escape uses its Bonus Action.
- Encounter start records creature sightings; attacks and special traits enter the campaign Bestiary only when observed. Knowledge persists and never reveals the full internal stat block.
