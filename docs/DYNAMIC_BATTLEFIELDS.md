## Outstanding and design limits
- Current DOM grid has a provisional 65,536-cell safety budget, not a permanent width or height cap. Larger maps should eventually use viewport culling or canvas.
- Cover uses sampled rays and object-kind height/cover profiles. Full 3D elevation, creature cover, all saving-throw interactions, interactive doors/windows and exhaustive spell/reaction integration need follow-up.
- Alternate tactical-engine geometry is compatible, but its camera has not yet received the complete classic-camera feature set. Verify roadside and ruins in live iPhone play, more gesture/edge-follow scenarios, and wider movement/attack regression coverage.
- Generator proves start/exit connectivity, not formal multiple-route diversity or tactical-interest scoring; positions are environment-specific and layouts vary. Explicit DM-provided geometry/positions are honored, but deeper exploration-to-battlefield continuity is outstanding.
- Illustrated reusable assets, subtle grid overlays and rendering performance improvements are next. Fog of war, concealment, advanced visibility, sound, and multi-floor exploration remain explicitly deferred.
- Keep original classic encounter UI, prior saves, and reward/character/inventory mechanics intact. Do not replace the combat interface.

- The shared physical object schema adds optional serialized locked, lockDC (10/15/20), lockComplexity (simple/complex), lockProgress, forceDC, material (glass/wood/stone/iron), hp and destroyed fields. Omitted fields preserve all existing saves and standard unlocked openings. Validation rejects contradictory or malformed fixtures. Generator adds an initially locked wooden door and a breakable glass window in ruins, and explicit glass HP in warehouse.
- Implements deterministic 2024 rules: DMG door defaults (wood AC15/18HP, metal AC19/72HP, stone AC17/40HP, glass AC13/4HP), DC15 normal lock picking with owned Thieves’ Tools, good/inferior/superior lock DC 15/10/20, one-action versus one-minute (ten-Utilize-action) locks, DC15 wood / 20 stone / 25 metal / 10 glass force checks, 2024 ability/skill/tool modifiers and advantage when both tool and skill are proficient, natural 1/20 D20 test outcomes, and dagger attack vs material AC (including extra critical damage die). Objects are immune to Poison/Psychic damage. Rolls are generated by the existing campaign rules; the underlying physical resolution is pure, deterministic, and covered by injected-roll tests.
- Locked doors cannot be opened with a free interaction until unlocked or forced. A successful lockpick spends an Action but still requires a separate free object interaction to swing the door; a successful force check spends an Action and opens the door. Dagger strikes spend the character's Action, update HP, and when destroyed remove collision and cover; shattered windows become difficult terrain. All physical states are persisted and immediately shared by pathfinding, sight/cover and combat adjudication. The one-free-interaction-per-turn resource remains separate and enforced.
- Kept the original classic practice encounter presentation and added a compact contextual fixture selector/actions inside it. Objects are directly targetable when nearby; unlocked openings still support their original one-tap behavior. Added locally bundled illustrated door-locked, door-broken and window-shattered variants; no external art URLs or fog of war.
- Deterministic tests check validation, lock checks, ten-action complex locks, force checks, tool prerequisites, character/action economy, cover/movement changes, attack AC/HP, natural 1/20 critical rules and save round-trips. iPhone browser tests check picking/opening a locked door, breaking a glass window, action/free-interaction economy, persistence and original combat UI.
