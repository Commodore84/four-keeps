## Final review

- Combined the former single-item Shields category with Armor; no armor subgroups are shown.
- Verified all 182 official catalog entries are assigned once, including ammunition cases and quivers, climbing equipment, specialist kits, exploration tools, and spellcasting foci.
- Preserved separate weapon categories, instruments, gaming sets, equipment packs, and potions.
- The largest category is Martial melee weapons (18), followed by Artisan tools (17); no oversized catch-all category remains.
- Merchants display only stocked primary categories. Magic-item category and rarity handling is unchanged.
